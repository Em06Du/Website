01 / MY ROLE

A year of responsibility

Ingenium Cabo Bianci is the Design & Engineering student association at HU University of Applied Sciences Utrecht. Alongside my engineering studies, I spent a year taking responsibility for the association’s relationships with industry.

About the association ↗

The point of contact for partners

As Public Relations board member, I was in contact with all the association’s partners and collaborating companies. I managed partner contracts, maintained relationships and organised activities between companies and our students. This included discussing the cooperation and its agreements with partners.

02 / PARTNERS & CONTRACTS

Negotiating and documenting relationships

I led contract negotiations with partners and succeeded in increasing partner contributions for the first time in ten years. I am proud of this result: it meant opening a conversation about established agreements and achieving something concrete for the association.

I also created a partner dossier documenting the partners the association had worked with. This recorded our partnership history as a foundation for future collaborations.

03 / NETWORKING DAYS

Two days of making connections

I organised two networking days, each attended by approximately 35 companies. Students could explore the professional world and speak directly with companies. For me, these events brought together partner relations, coordination and event organisation.

04 / STUDY TRIP

Bringing back the study trip

One of my favourite memories is helping to organise the study trip. After a three-year gap, we organised it again. Working with others to make this trip happen for the association was a particularly rewarding part of my board year.

What I took from this year

This board year gave me experience in maintaining professional relationships, negotiating and organising. I was responsible for agreements with external partners while working with my fellow board members. I bring that experience to engineering: understanding the technology and coordinating with the people around it.
