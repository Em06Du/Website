INTERNSHIP / GOOILAND ELEKTRO / 2025

Climate control.
Its own controller.

During my twenty-week internship at Gooiland Elektro, I developed an independent Fan Coil Unit controller, including an interface for operation and monitoring.

My role Research, control software & HMI
Context Individual internship assignment with technical guidance

IN THIS PROJECT

01 / The challenge 02 / My contribution 03 / The outcome 04 / Technology
CODESYS HMI Modbus TCP Remote I/O Automation

01 / THE CHALLENGE

The challenge

A Fan Coil Unit heats or cools a room. When multiple units depend on one central PLC, a controller problem can affect several rooms. My task was to research and realise a locally operating solution with a clear operator interface.

02 / MY CONTRIBUTION

My contribution

  1. I compared hardware options and communication protocols and supported my recommendations with a cost overview.

  2. I developed the controller in CODESYS and designed the HMI for automatic and manual operation, temperature settings and alarms.

  3. I connected the Weintek HMI with CODESYS to Weidmüller remote I/O using Modbus TCP, and delivered a manual, wiring diagram, test plan and development recommendations.

03 / THE OUTCOME

The outcome

The project produced a working setup combining the FCU controls and operator interface. The documentation supports handover and further development.

04 / A CLOSER LOOK

A closer look at the technology

HMI + CODESYS+

A Weintek cMT2058XH with CODESYS provides the operator interface and local control logic. It communicates with Weidmüller remote I/O over Modbus TCP.

Control conditions+

The application controls the fan, cooling valve and heater. The design includes fan-running conditions before heating can start and a fan run-on period.

From cost research to test setup+

The original hardware target was below €500. An investigated M120E concept came to approximately €350, but was not the realised setup. At the supervisor’s request, available Weidmüller I/O was used; the presentation estimates this combination at approximately €1,300.

What I learned

I gained experience across the development process, from requirements and component selection to programming, wiring and handover. I also learned to balance software structure with the needs of the application and to respond when project decisions change the original plan.
