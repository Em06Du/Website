STUDY PROJECT / FACTORY AUTOMATION

One production line.
Working together.

A production line works when each step connects to the next. In this third-year project, I worked on designing a line to feed pallets, remove cans and stack empty pallets. I did this project together with three other students.

01 / THE CHALLENGE

The challenge

The line processes pallets of cans in a defined sequence. Buffers manage incoming pallets, a depalletizer removes cans layer by layer and a palletizer collects the empty pallets. Sensors and drives must work together at the right moment.

02 / HOW THE LINE WORKS

How the line works

  1. Feed and buffer

    A pallet enters on the roller conveyor. Five buffers only pass it on when the next section has space.

  2. Remove the cans

    The depalletizer lifts the pallet. A vacuum system removes the cardboard sheets before a pusher transfers each layer of cans onto the outfeed conveyor.

  3. Stack empty pallets

    The empty pallet moves to the palletizer. Its lift stacks the pallets so a forklift can remove them together.

03 / FROM SEQUENCE TO DESIGN

From sequence to design

The project documents describe the machine sequence, component selection, electrical design and mechanical design. These elements need to work as one line, from sensor positions and motor selection to the handover between stations.

04 / A CLOSER LOOK

A closer look at the technology

Sensors and sequence+

Photoelectric sensors check pallet presence and position. Distance sensors monitor lift height. Each step depends on the state of the next station.

Drives and movement+

The documentation describes conveyor motors, servo drives for the lifts and linear actuators for components including gates and gripping pins.

Safety in the design+

The design considers guarding, emergency stops, access for pallet removal and a brake on the depalletizer lift. These are design provisions; the 3D model does not demonstrate a validated safety function.
